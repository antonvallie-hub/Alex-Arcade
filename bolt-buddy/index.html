Parent corner

Privacy: the microphone is on only while the big button is held. Speech is matched on this tablet against Bolt's short word list (hi, dance, jump, sing, name, colors, animals, rocket, sleep, joke, the child's name and Bolt). Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. No ads, no links, no purchases. Progress is saved on this device only.

Voice clips say "Alex" and "Bolt" only while those names are set; with other names Bolt uses friendly lines without a name.
