Parent corner

Privacy: in hands-free mode the microphone turns on when Alex taps Bolt and turns off when Bolt falls asleep (about 20 seconds of quiet), when you leave the talk screen or the app goes to the background. Bolt ignores the microphone while he is talking. In hold-to-talk mode it is on only while the button is held. Speech is matched on this tablet against Bolt's word list; nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. No ads, no links, no purchases. Progress is saved on this device only.

Voice clips say "Alex" and "Bolt" only while those names are set; with other names Bolt uses friendly lines without a name.
